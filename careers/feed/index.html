]]> We’re looking for reliable, detail-oriented Production Assistants to support our team during the on-site execution of events. This is an as-needed, event-based position, not a full-time role, and is ideal for individuals who enjoy working in a fast-paced environment and being part of the team that brings events to life. Onsite shifts typically last between four and 12 hours and include nights and weekends.

Production Assistants work alongside our production team to help ensure events run smoothly from setup through breakdown. Responsibilities may include assisting with event setup, managing event materials, supporting vendor coordination, directing guests, providing general logistical support throughout the event, and breakdown. 

Responsibilities may include:

·       Assisting with event setup and breakdown

·       Helping manage and distribute event materials and supplies

·       Supporting vendor load-in and load-out

·       Assisting with guest flow and directional support

·       Helping maintain the organization of production areas

·       Providing general logistical support to the production team as needed

The ideal candidate is organized, proactive, comfortable taking direction, and able to remain calm and helpful in a dynamic event environment.

Requirements

·       Ability to work on your feet for extended periods

·       Strong communication and teamwork skills

·       Positive attitude and willingness to help where needed

·       Availability for evening and weekend events

·       Ability to lift and carry up to 20lbs

This role offers the opportunity to gain hands-on experience working behind the scenes of professional events and supporting a team dedicated to delivering exceptional experiences.

The Designer owns design for all events on which they are assigned as the lead designer. Responsible for creative research and creative construction. Is accountable to budget parameters. Provides creative instruction for event development and execution. Effectively works with internal teams regarding design and production. Fosters client relationships through client contact at a design level. Works closely with the Design Director to ensure all design work is of the highest quality, up to company standards and on brand for the client. Leads and directs the work of others. A wide degree of creativity and flexibility is expected.

Responsibilities

  • Provides design and overall creative direction to all assigned client projects.
  • Provides input and recommendations on venues and suppliers to be presented to the client.
  • Develops and communicates the event concept and design, specifically in the details such as theme, composition and integration of multi-faceted components of an event such as venue(s), color, sound, lighting, décor, and any other relevant attributes of the creative planning process
  • Meets with clients and with internal teams to understand the scope of the event.
  • Ensures designs are completed on time and adhere to the established specifications, standards and budgets.
  • Presents and promotes event concept and design to clients
  • Performs site visits to all selected sites for assigned client events
  • Leads calls and provides design input with outside suppliers
  • Oversees the development of a Design Planning Timeline based on the Event Planning Timeline.
  • Offers design direction for floor plans and renderings
  • Provides content for design presentations and participates in presentation
  • Provides ballpark pricing to internal teams for recommended rental, linen and décor items
  • Reviews and approves floral sample photos and stationery proofs
  • Selects service-ware design based on menu selection
  • Provides design direction and communicates details of production to event teams and vendors, to ensure product implementation is executed at the highest level
  • Collaborates closely with event teams and vendors, communicating all relevant event design changes or modifications through to final production detail including set-up, rehearsal, event production and post event activities with all staff in the creation and finalization of the event plan
  • Consistently deliver on brand, elevated design concepts, while adhering to established budgets and production timelines
  • Directs lighting look for Design Presentations and participates in Design Presentation setup
  • Working with the Inventory and Production  groups, responsible for creative specialty builds and communicates all information and a plan for execution to the internal Production team
  • Responsible for all design implementation on site at client events
  • Participates in weekly Department brainstorms and meetings to share knowledge within the department.
  • Responsible for new supplier research in the categories of bands/specialty entertainment, photographers, videographers, stationers, and graphic designers

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree
  • A minimum of 7-10 years of experience in the creative field required, specifically working with high profile clients, non-profit organizations and event concept and design
  • Experience working with high profile brands or clients preferred
  • A solid track record of increasing responsibility in a creative role
  • The ability to work independently within the culture of strong collaboration in a group process
  • Relies on extensive experience and judgment to plan and accomplish goals
  • Works autonomously with minimal supervision
  • Exceptional presentation skills coupled with sales acumen to promote the company Class A brand standards and its services
  • Attention to detail, critical thinking, creative ability, and willingness to do ‘whatever it takes’ to achieve the project objective
  • Able to work in a fast-paced environment and work effectively under pressure
  • Exceptional organization, communication and project/time management skills
  • Flexibility to change and adapt to considerable variety in the workplace
  • Knowledge of textiles, décor, architecture, lighting, production processes, quality control, costs, and other techniques for maximizing the efficiency of the event plan
  • Ability to travel (approximately 25%)
  • Proficient in the Adobe Creative Suite. Working knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook preferred
  • Able to work overtime, nights and weekends

]]> Description

The Rafanelli Events internship program will offer each participant the opportunity to gain an in-depth understanding of many facets of the event planning process and how the Rafanelli team creates, designs, and implements its “one of a kind” events. Interns will participate in  weekly master classes that will help them to gain knowledge about the event industry. 

Semester Curriculum

Throughout the course of your internship, you will gain a combination of virtual and hands-on educational experiences. Those include but are not limited to:

  • New Intern Orientation and Process Review
  • Weekly “Master Classes” Reviewing Key Event Documents, Design Development and Deep Diving into Rafanelli Processes
  • Behind the Scenes Pre-event Production Assistance
  • Weekly “Office Hours”
  • Assisting with On-Site Event Production and Execution

Eligibility Requirements:

  • 10 hour weekly virtual commitment
  • Set schedule per week
  • Unpaid position – you must be enrolled in school and eligible to receive university credit or required to complete an internship for your degree

Application Deadlines:

Winter/Spring Semester

  • Semester Schedule: January through May
  • Submission Deadline: November 30th

Summer Semester

  • Semester Schedule: May through August
  • Submission Deadline: March 30th

Fall Semester

  • Semester Schedule: September through December
  • Submission Deadline: July 31st
